Shiffrar & Freyd (1990) — Apparent motion of the human body

Two pictures alternate (picture 1, blank, picture 2, blank, ...) as in a tachistoscope. Stimulus duration (SD), blank interstimulus interval (ISI) and the stimulus pair were varied. The observer watched for as many cycles as desired and then marked the answer sheet.
Watch the alternating pictures for as many cycles as you like, then indicate the path of motion on the answer sheet (options: A, B, A&B or other). Sometimes the path may be very clear, sometimes there may be several paths, or no motion. (The buttons are active only during a trial block. The original sheet showed a line drawing of the stimulus with paths A and B diagrammed; those drawings are not reproduced. Responses are kept in memory only and are never shown or scored.)

Methods (Shiffrar & Freyd, 1990, Psychological Science 1(4), 257–264). Photographs cannot be reproduced here: body pairs are simple drawn figures; frame aspect follows the figures per stimulus family (measured on Fig. 1 and Fig. 3): arm/hand/head photos about 0.98 (page 520 x 530), leg photos about 0.86 (520 x 605), clock photos about 0.90 (520 x 580), rectangles unspecified (assumed 0.90, 520 x 578); the picture height is drawn at an assumed 12 deg, set in Display setup, and the width follows the family aspect with a plain background; poses are approximations (Fig. 1 shows only two example pairs; the leg pair is cropped below the waist, the arm pair above the thigh). The actual stimuli were full-colour photographs of a model, stationary except for one or two limbs. Clocks were photographs of a real wall clock. On the original answer sheet path A was the shortest path and path B a longer path between the two positions; in Exp. 1 path A was always the physically/anatomically impossible path and path B the longer, possible one (p. 260). The diagrams themselves are not reproduced.

Within a block, SOAs of a pair are shown consecutively in ascending or descending order (the paper says only that SOA order was ascending for some observers and descending for others; ordering of sheets across pairs is not stated); 1000 ms blank between trials is assumed; a trial ends when the observer presses an answer. The two-session or between-subject counterbalancing (5 ascending / 4 or 5 descending) is not enforced.258-262
Timing implementationThe refresh rate is measured at load (median of ~60 rAF intervals); every duration is converted to a whole number of frames at that rate and scheduled by counting animation frames; realised ms and a non-whole-frame warning are shown. E.g. the 20 ms ISI of the head pair is 1 frame (16.7 ms) at 60 Hz. The tachistoscope itself had exact timing.—

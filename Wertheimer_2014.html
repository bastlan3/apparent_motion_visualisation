Methods

The source paper (Wertheimer 1912, via the 2014 reconstruction) was not available when this page was built, so no value or page reference could be checked. Only the following is taken as established from secondary knowledge: two stimuli (lines) were exposed one after the other at two separate positions, the time interval between them was varied by the experimenter, and observers gave free verbal descriptions. Every numeric value below is an ASSUMPTION, not a value from the paper.

StimulusTwo vertical bars, 0.2 x 3 deg, dark (#111) on light grey (#f0f0f0). Shape, size, contrast: ASSUMPTION.
PositionsCentres 6 deg apart, same height; first at left, second at right. ASSUMPTION.
Viewing distance57 cm. ASSUMPTION (paper value unknown). Screen scale from the Display setup control (default 37.8 px/cm); stimuli are drawn in degrees.
Exposure80 ms per bar, rounded to a whole number of frames at the measured refresh rate. ASSUMPTION.
IntervalBlank between offset of bar 1 and onset of bar 2 (ISI, not SOA). Levels 0, 30, 60, 100, 150, 200, 400 ms: ASSUMPTION; each converted to whole frames at the measured rate. Realised values are shown in the display line.
TimingRefresh rate measured at load (median of ~60 requestAnimationFrame intervals); sequence scheduled by counting frames.
TrialBlock: each interval level 2 times (ASSUMPTION), random order. A trial is one exposure pair (bar 1, blank, bar 2, blank field); it ends when the experimenter presses Next trial after the observer's verbal report. Inter-trial blank field of 1 s. ASSUMPTION.
FixationNone shown; unspecified.
ResponseFree verbal report by the observer in the original; no response interface on this page. Trial order and levels are logged internally only.
Not specified hereLuminance, field size, observers' instructions, total number of trials, other figures (e.g. line plus angle), other separations and orientations.

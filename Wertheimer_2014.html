Methods

The source paper (Wertheimer 1912, via the 2014 reconstruction) was not available when this page was built. Only the following is taken as established: two stimuli (lines) were exposed one after the other at two separate positions, the time interval between them was varied by the experimenter, and observers gave free verbal descriptions. Every numeric value below is an ASSUMPTION, not a value from the paper.

StimulusTwo vertical bars, 8 x 120 px, dark on light grey. Shape, size, contrast: ASSUMPTION.
PositionsCentres 240 px apart, same height; first at left, second at right. ASSUMPTION. No viewing distance given, so no visual angle.
Exposure5 frames per bar (83 ms at 60 Hz). ASSUMPTION.
Interval0 to 30 blank frames (0 to 500 ms at 60 Hz), set by the slider; the interval is the blank between offset of bar 1 and onset of bar 2 (ISI, not SOA). Levels: ASSUMPTION. Frames are counted per display refresh; realised durations shown at right assume 60 Hz.
TrialOne exposure pair per press of Present (bar 1, blank, bar 2, blank field). Optional repeat re-presents the pair after a 30-frame blank: ASSUMPTION.
FixationNone shown; unspecified.
ResponseFree verbal report by the observer in the original; no response interface on this page.
Not specified hereLuminance, field size, observers' instructions, number of trials, other figures (e.g. line plus angle), other separations.

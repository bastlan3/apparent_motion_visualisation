Attneave & Block (1973) — Experiment II (pictorial depth)

Two small red lights alternate in a clear central alley of a drawn grid. Stimulus reconstruction of Experiment II (Perception & Psychophysics 13, 301–307). Experiments I and III used physical lights in real space and are not reproduced. View in a dim room, one eye, at the viewing distance set below.
Watch the lights while fixating the indicated region. Press Movement (or the space bar) when the lights appear to move back and forth. Adopt as stable a criterion as possible for “good” (believable) movement. You may have to wait an irregular number of trials before it appears.

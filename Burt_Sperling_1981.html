Burt & Sperling (1981) - multiple-path motion stimulus

Methods (Psychol. Rev. 88, 171-195)

StimulusOne horizontal row of evenly spaced dots, flashed repeatedly; between presentations the row moves horizontally by H and downward by V; the stack of rows sweeps through a rectangular window (p. 173-174, Fig. 1a).
Dot separation D17 mm (1.70 cm) = 29.3 arcmin at 2 m (p. 174)
Row shift H, V (Exp 1)H = +/-6.8 mm (11.7 arcmin), V = 1.8 mm (3.09 arcmin) (p. 174, Table 1)
Interflash interval tExp 1: ten values, logarithmic steps, 12-56 ms (p. 175). Exp 2/3: seven values per configuration around the transition (about 8-40 ms in Table 2); the seven values are not listed in the paper - free input here.
FlashEach row presentation = 2 refreshes of a fast P4 phosphor, 3 ms apart (p. 175). Here: one display frame per presentation.
Control stimuli (Exp 1)M1: every other dot removed (dot separation 2D, same H, V, t). M2: every other row removed (H, V, t doubled, separation D). (Table 1)
Exp 1 trialAmbiguous M1,2 alternated with the control three times, beginning with M1 (P1 trials), 1 s pause between stimuli; mirror images on half the trials; rating 0-6 at the end (p. 175). P2 trials: the paper does not state which stimulus comes first; control first assumed. Authors as observers, 40 trials per session (4 motion types x 10 t).
Exp 1 rating scale0 = undetectable; 1 = 20% as strong as the reference; ... 5 = equal strength; 6 = stronger in M1,2 than in the control (p. 175).
Window and fixationRows within a 22 cm x 6 cm area (6.3 x 1.72 deg at 2 m); bottom of area 4 cm (1.15 deg) above the fixation point; observers fixate carefully (p. 174-175).
Viewing2 m (Exp 1, 3), 1 m and 2 m (Exp 2); dark room, display surface 0.058 cd/m2; dot energy about 1.6e-6 cd-s per refresh (p. 175).
Exp 218 configurations, D = 17 mm; H, V pairs of Table 2 (list in the menu). One sweep of the row through the window per trial (max 3 s); observer reports whether dominant motion was to the right or left; 7 t values, reflections interleaved (p. 177-181).
Exp 3Same as Exp 2 with dots, rings, alternating dots/rings, or alternating orthogonal bars; D = 17 mm, H = 6.8 mm, V = 1.84, 2.45, 3.06 mm, 2 m; P1-P2 geometry (p. 181-184).
AssumptionsDot diameter, ring and bar sizes (not given in text; drawn here as about 2 mm dot, 3.3 mm ring, 3.3 mm bar); on-screen scale 3 px/mm so the window is 660 x 180 px (physical size and viewing distance depend on your screen); equal brightness for all elements (paper gives different energies); 500 ms fixation before each stimulus; start of the sweep at the top of the window; one sweep = V-step count to the window bottom (Exp 1 length unspecified).

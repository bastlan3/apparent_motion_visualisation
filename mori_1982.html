Mori (1982) — Probe judgement task for an apparent-motion line

Line AB flashes, then line A′B′, then after a blank a dim probe line CD flashes once. On each trial you judge the probe's orientation (or height) relative to the line you saw moving at the probe's horizontal position.
Choose a block and press Start. Look at the screen, watch the moving line, then judge the briefly flashed probe.

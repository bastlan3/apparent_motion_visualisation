Mori (1982) — Probe judgement task for an apparent-motion line

Line AB flashes, then line A′B′, then after a blank a dim probe line CD flashes once. On each trial you judge the probe's orientation (or height) relative to the line you saw moving at the probe's horizontal position.
Choose a block and press Start. Look at the screen, watch the moving line, then judge the briefly flashed probe.

Frame rateRefresh rate measured at load (median requestAnimationFrame interval); every duration is converted to a whole number of frames at that rate and scheduled by counting frames. Realised durations are shown in the Display setup panel; a warning appears when a paper duration is not a whole number of frames. The 10 ms probe becomes at least one whole frame (16.7 ms at 60 Hz). To keep probe energy near the paper's, its luminance is scaled by 10 ms / realised duration (Bloch's law, assumed), on top of the 2.6/5.8 ratio. If the refresh cannot be measured (hidden tab, 3 s timeout), 60 Hz is assumed and a note is shown; measurement is retried when the tab becomes visible. Frames are counted from rAF timestamps, so a lag of 2-5 frames does not lengthen a phase.

Hecht & Proffitt (1991) — Apparent extended body motions in depth

JEP: Human Perception & Performance 17(4), 1090–1103. Experiments 1–5: two "domino" objects alternate on screen; the observer places a probe on the invisible system axis and then gives the experiment's rating or sets a depth plane. Responses are not stored or analysed here.

Viewing distanceabout 60 cm (Exps 1–2; 12.7° between domino centres); 64 cm (Exps 3–4); 25 cm (Exp 5). Exps 3–4: black viewing box, window occluding screen borders, monocular. Exp 5: box removed, monocular, dark room. Not reproducible on a monitor; stated for reference.pp.1094, 1098, 1101
Domino3.2 x 1.6 x 0.8 cm (Exp 3: 2.6 x 1.3 x 0.65 cm); top marked with dots, ends with lines, sides colour-coded; long axis along the object rayp.1094; p.1099. Dot count (3), line count (2) and colours assumed.
GeometryTwo fixed dominos, centres 13.5 cm apart on screen (Exp 3: 6.75 cm), placed symmetrically about the invisible system axis on object rays from origin O, angle theta to the axis. Internal rotations alpha1 (about x', long axis), alpha2 (about y'); alpha3 = 0. System rotated by beta1 (external x = system axis) or beta2 (y); beta3 = in-plane orientation of the axis.pp.1094–1095, Fig. 4. Rotation sign, rotation order, and applying the same alpha to both dominos are assumptions.
ProjectionParallel (orthographic) in Exps 1–4; polar (perspective) at 25 cm in Exp 5pp.1095–1099, 1101. Exp 5 eye on screen-centre normal; depth zero at the screen plane.
3-D theta and distanceB1: tan(theta3D) = tan(theta2D)/cos(beta1), distance = 13.5/cos(beta1). B2: distance 13.5; theta3D from theta2D (relation garbled in the PDF; I used tan(theta3D) = tan(theta2D) cos(beta2), which reproduces the Exp 5 values).Appendix pp.1102–1103; B2 form inferred
Origin O and axis orientationO randomised within a 4-cm circle at screen centre; axis beta3 randomp.1094. 4 cm taken as the diameter; beta3 uniform over 360° (paper: random within each 90° sector in Exp 1)
TimingEach domino 220 ms on, SOA 500 ms, cycle (D1-ISI-D2-ISI) 1000 ms, continuous. Realised at 60 Hz by counting frames: 13 frames on (216.7 ms), 17 frames ISI (283.3 ms), SOA 30 frames, cycle 60 frames. Screens not near 60 Hz fall back to a 60 Hz logical frame clock.p.1094
ProbeTurned on by the observer; flashes in the ISIs for 160 ms (10 frames = 166.7 ms at 60 Hz); starts at a random extreme end of the system axis near the screen border; moved continuously along the axis. Window = two pointed triangles separated enough for the domino to pass; or a third domino at an intermediate position and orientation.p.1094. Probe onset 3 frames after domino offset; gap 4 cm (3.2 cm in Exp 3); triangle size; probe domino drawn with the system-axis orientation and the stimulus alpha values; slider/keys as control device (Exp 2 ratings used a thumb wheel).
Exp 1 (pp.1094–1095)theta = 30°, beta1 = beta2 = 0; alpha1, alpha2 each 0, 15, 30, 45, 60° (25 trials per block); each trial once with window, once with domino probe (order counterbalanced); random order in block; four practice trials; goodness scale (good–poor) after placement; break after half the trials.p.1094–1095
Exp 2 (pp.1095–1098)15 stimuli: 2D (alpha1 = alpha2 = 20°; theta 30° or 35°), B1 (beta1 34.5, 36.1, 50.9, 62.3, 72.2°; 2D theta 3.1, 7.1, 12.9, 20.6, 25.4°), B2 (beta2 48.8, 60.3, 61.0, 67.8, 72.2°; 2D theta 10, 15, 20, 25, 30°), alpha = 0 for B1/B2. Each shown twice (window block, domino block; 30 trials, break after 15). Probe moves with the system orientation. Two ratings, continuous 0–100 thumb wheel: difficulty (easy–difficult) and depth (in depth–in plane).pp.1095–1098. Which 2D pairs use 30 vs 35 not stated (I used 30, 30, 30, 35, 35). The 2D theta values are taken as the projected angles the Appendix formula starts from.
Exp 3 (pp.1098–1099)Same 15 stimuli at half size, 64 cm, viewing box, monocular. One block per observer, half with window, half with domino probe. After placement a key shows a regularly spaced grid drawn in perspective; it can be turned in 5° steps about a vertical or a horizontal axis through its centre (not both). Rating scales dropped.pp.1098–1099. Grid 24 x 24 cm, 3-cm spacing, starts frontoparallel; probe hidden once grid appears; no fixed number of practice trials beyond four.
Exp 4 (pp.1099–1101)Display size as Exp 1–2, viewing as Exp 3. System rotated about x (B1) or y (B2) by 30 or 60°; domino orientation parallel to the object ray, alpha1 = 30, alpha2 = 30, or both 30. Domino probe only; grid task.p.1099–1101. The text lists four orientations but counts 12 stimuli; I present all four (16). theta2D = 30° assumed.
Exp 5 (p.1101)As Exp 4 without box; 25 cm; polar projection; 9 stimuli: B1 beta1 30/45/60 (theta3D 33.7, 39.2, 49.1°), B2 beta2 30/45/60 (theta3D 26.6, 22.2, 16.1°), 2D (theta 16.1, 32.7, 49.1°); alpha1 = alpha2 = 10°.p.1101

Proffitt, Gilden, Kaiser & Whelan (1988) — configural orientation & perceived AM trajectory

Two rectangles blink in different locations and orientations (a single rotation apart). The observer slides a blinking probe along the system axis onto the perceived motion path.
Watch the apparent motion until the path is clear, then turn the probe on and slide it (drag on the canvas or ↑/↓) until it lies on the perceived path. Toggle it off to check the path is unchanged.

Faithful reconstruction of the apparatus in Proffitt, Gilden, Kaiser & Whelan (1988), Perception & Psychophysics, 43(5), 465–474. Two fixed test rectangles (a single rotation about origin O apart) blink with an observer-moved probe interleaved: test1, probe, test2, probe (70 ms each, 180 ms ISI); ~60 cm viewing, centre separation 13.3 cm = 12.6°. Geometry: system axis = perpendicular bisector of the two centres (angle β); arm = O→centre at ±θ (orientation change = 2θ); α = major axis vs arm. The probe slides along the system axis with fixed orientation (β+α); placement is scored 0 (straight chord midpoint) → 100 (circular arc apex, deviation (sep/2)·tan(θ/2)). Rectangle ends are coloured red (AB) / blue (CD) to fix directedness — the only orientation cue near θ=90°. The perceived path is deliberately not drawn; O / system axis / reference straight & circular paths appear only under the construction overlay. Exp 3 swaps rectangles for a patterned disk (6 internal lines, red/blue halves, magenta outline) or an unpatterned disk.

Proffitt, Gilden, Kaiser & Whelan (1988) — configural orientation & apparent-motion path

Two fixed rectangles (or disks) blink alternately in different places and orientations. The observer turns on a blinking probe, moves it along a fixed line, and presses a key when done. In Exp 2 and Exp 3 the observer then also rates the goodness of the path.

Methods (paper values; amber = unspecified, my assumption)

DisplayTektronix 4129, screen 30 cm high x 35 cm wide, 4 lines/mm (p. 467). Viewing distance approximately 60 cm (p. 468): 13.3 cm separation = 12.6°. Drawn in degrees from your calibrated px/cm and viewing distance (see Display setup): sizes are the paper's cm at 60 cm converted to degrees, so the canvas is 35 x 30 cm at 60 cm (≈ 33 x 28°) whatever the distance. Default 37.8 px/cm (not calibrated); background uniform gray (stated for Exp 3, p. 472; used for all); luminances and colours unspecified. If the canvas does not fit your window the page scrolls: the stimulus is scrolled to the middle of the window at each trial start and to the goodness scale when it appears; instructions are shown as an overlay at the bottom of the window. The stimulus is never shrunk (zoom the browser out or sit further away if you want it all visible, then recalibrate). Measuring refresh…
Test stimuliTwo fixed rectangles 4.1 x 0.8 (printed "mm", p. 467; drawn as cm, since mm would be implausibly small), centres 13.3 cm apart (12.6°). Lines AB and CD (short ends) red and blue. Outline (light 1.5 px), end-line width (4 px), red/blue values, and which end is red (the +major-axis side) are not stated.
Geometryβ = system-axis angle from horizontal (set {45, 135, 224, 315}°; printed 224, used as printed; random, never equal on successive trials). Arms start at P and make angle θ with the system axis; each rectangle's major axis makes angle α with its arm; same α, θ for both rectangles (orientation change 2θ; p. 467, Fig. 3). P varies randomly within a 4-cm circle at screen centre for each stimulus (p. 468). Circle taken as radius 2 cm. With P as rotation origin the centres are R = 6.65/sin θ from P (25.7 cm at θ = 15°), which leaves the 35 x 30 cm screen for small θ. The page therefore places P itself in the circle when both rectangles fit on screen (up to 40 random tries), otherwise places the chord midpoint of the two centres in the circle; the rule used, P and the midpoint are logged per trial. Test 1 (flashes first) is on the +θ arm; α is added counter-clockwise to the arm angle (sign convention unstated).
ProbeSame shape, orientation (β+α) and colouring as the test rectangles; moves continuously along the system axis only. At trial start it is OFF, at the left or right border of the screen; the observer must switch it on (p. 468). All β are diagonals, so an axis through the centre may meet the top or bottom edge first; "left"/"right" here means the end of the axis with the smaller/larger screen x, which is then clipped to the screen edge.
TimingSequence test1, probe, test2, probe, repeat; each stimulus 70 ms, ISI 180 ms, the same whether the probe is on or off (probe slots stay blank) (p. 468). Frame-counted from the measured refresh (median of ~60 frames); the realised ms are given in the display line above. Loops until the observer finishes; no fixation mark (none mentioned). Late frames (interval over 1.5x nominal) are counted per trial and exported.
TaskGet a good path first with the probe off, then turn the probe on and move it onto the path, then turn it off and on to check the path is unchanged (p. 468). A key signals no further adjustment; timing runs from the first probe-moving key to that key (observers not told; p. 469). Exp 1 (p. 467-468) describes probe placement only: no goodness scale and no timing in this session (the page skips the rating phase and records neither). In Exp 2 and Exp 3 (p. 469-470) a goodness scale then appears at the bottom of the screen; observers rate confidence in the placement and clarity of the path with a thumbscrew-moved marker, with the display still blinking (p. 469-470). Keys: Space = probe on/off, ←/→ = move along the axis toward screen left/right (0.12 cm per frame at 60 Hz, Shift = 0.02), Enter = done (requires the probe to have been switched on and moved at least once); scale endpoints labelled "poor"/"good"; marker starts mid-scale and moves with ←/→ (0.008 per frame, Shift 0.002) in place of the thumbscrew.
Exp 1 (p. 467-468)12 observers. α/θ = 0/20, 0/22, 0/24, 90/20. Practice: 4 further stimuli (the rest of Foster's 8) x 2 blocks, random order, probe side random in block 1 and opposite in block 2. Test: same, with 4 blocks; initial side random in block 1, then opposite to the previous showing of that stimulus (L,R,L,R or reverse). Rest halfway through, with an unrelated task. The practice values are not given: 0/18, 0/26, 90/22, 0/28 are invented neighbours. β set as in Exp 2 (not stated). Distractor task: five arithmetic sums (placeholder). Order within block random.
Exp 2 (p. 469-470)12 naive observers (6 M, 6 F). α = 0, -15, 30, -45, 60, -75, 90°; θ = 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90°; initial probe L/R; β random as above. Procedure identical to Exp 1. One trial per cell (84), random order (repetitions unstated); practice as for Exp 1 (invented values); rest and distractor at the halfway point.
Exp 3 (p. 472)24 naive students in two groups of 12. Two disks 2 cm diameter (1.9°), magenta outline. Patterned: six equally spaced parallel lines, one semicircle red, the other blue, gray between; α = 0 or 90, θ = 30 only; start L/R (patterned block = 4 trials). Unpatterned: outline only; L/R start only (block = 2 trials). Two blocks per observer, patterned first when both are viewed. Exposed group: practice with patterned disks, patterned block, rest with a distractor task, unpatterned block. Naive group: one practice block, then two test blocks, all unpatterned. Reading of "two blocks" as one patterned plus one unpatterned block for the exposed group is an interpretation. Practice block for the exposed group = one patterned block. Line direction: perpendicular to the major axis (parallel to the rectangle's short ends). Unpatterned disk geometry: θ = 30, β random as in Exp 2.
ParticipantsExp 1: 12 (6 M, 6 F); Exp 2: 12 (gender between subjects); Exp 3: 24. Not applicable here.

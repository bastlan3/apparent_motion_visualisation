Proffitt, Gilden, Kaiser & Whelan (1988) — configural orientation & apparent-motion path

Two fixed rectangles (or disks) blink alternately in different places and orientations. The observer turns on a blinking probe, moves it along a fixed line, and presses a key when done, then rates the goodness of the path.
α θ β stim start

Methods (paper values; amber = unspecified, my assumption)

DisplayTektronix 4129, screen 30 cm high x 35 cm wide (p. 467). Canvas = that screen at 20 px/cm (700x600). Viewing ~60 cm, so 1 cm ≈ 0.95°; the real visual angle depends on your screen and distance. Background: uniform gray (stated for Exp 3, p. 472; used for all); luminances and colours unspecified.
Test stimuliTwo fixed rectangles 4.1 x 0.8 (printed "mm", p. 467; drawn as cm, since mm would be implausibly small), centres 13.3 cm apart (≈12.6°). Lines AB and CD (short ends) coloured red and blue. Outline, line widths and colours unspecified; red end on the +major-axis side.
Geometryβ = system-axis angle from horizontal (set {45, 135, 224, 315}°; printed 224, drawn as 224 as printed; random, never equal on successive trials). θ = arm angle from system axis, α = major axis vs arm; same for both rectangles (orientation change 2θ). P is placed randomly within a 4-cm circle at screen centre each stimulus. Taken as radius 2 cm; P is placed at the midpoint of the two centres, because with P at the rotation origin the centres fall off the 35 x 30 cm screen for small θ. Test 1 on the +θ arm.
ProbeSame shape, orientation (β+α) and colouring as the test rectangles; moves continuously along the system axis only. At trial start it is OFF, at the left or right screen border; the observer must switch it on (p. 468).
TimingSequence test1, probe, test2, probe, repeat; each stimulus 70 ms, ISI 180 ms, the same whether the probe is on or off (probe slots stay blank). Frame-counted: at 60 Hz = 4 frames (66.7 ms) and 11 frames (183.3 ms). Measuring refresh… Loops until the observer finishes; no fixation mark (none mentioned).
TaskGet a good path first with the probe off, then turn the probe on and move it onto the path, then turn it off and on to check the path is unchanged (p. 468). A key signals no further adjustment; timing runs from the first probe-moving key to that key (observers not told). Then a goodness scale appears at the bottom of the screen, a marker is moved by a thumbscrew, with the display still blinking (p. 470). Keys: Space = probe on/off, ←/→ = move along the axis toward screen left/right (Shift = fine), Enter = done; scale endpoints labelled "poor"/"good", marker starts mid-scale, ←/→ stand in for the thumbscrew.
Exp 1 (p. 467-468)α/θ = 0/20, 0/22, 0/24, 90/20. 4 test blocks x 4 stimuli; probe start side random for block 1, then opposite to the previous showing of that stimulus (L,R,L,R or reverse). Order within block random. The paper's 4 practice stimuli (the rest of Foster's 8) are not specified and are omitted. β set as in Exp 2.
Exp 2 (p. 469-470)α = 0, -15, 30, -45, 60, -75, 90°; θ = 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90°; initial probe L/R; β random as above. One trial per cell (84), random order, repetitions unstated; rest at the halfway point (unrelated task, not implemented).
Exp 3 (p. 472)Two disks 2 cm diameter (≈1.9°), magenta outline. Patterned: six equally spaced parallel lines, one semicircle red, the other blue, gray between; α = 0 or 90, θ = 30 only; start L/R. Unpatterned: outline only; L/R start only. Two blocks per group: exposed group = patterned blocks then unpatterned blocks; naive group = unpatterned only (one practice block, then two test blocks). Line direction: parallel to the rectangle's short ends. Unpatterned disk geometry: θ = 30, random β. Practice block and block counts of the exposed group as stated: 2 patterned + 2 unpatterned.
ParticipantsExp 1: 12 (6 M, 6 F); Exp 2: gender between-subjects; Exp 3: 24 naive students in two groups of 12. Not applicable here.

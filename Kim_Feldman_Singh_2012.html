Kim, Feldman & Singh (2012) — curved apparent motion induced by amodal completion

A red rectangle alternates between the two ends of a stationary semicircular tube shown against a random-dot background. After 3½ cycles the display vanishes and the observer reports whether the motion went along a straight or a curved path, then rates motion quality.

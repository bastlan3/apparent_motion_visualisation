Navon (1976) — Ambiguous apparent-motion episodes made of letters, Hebrew letters or line segments

Each episode is a cycle of 2 or 6 brief arrays of elements, repeated. The observer fixates the centre dot, watches as long as the first interpretation holds, and presses a key when it switches to another.
Press Start trial (the experimenter's key). A 1-s warning beep sounds with the fixation dot on; then the episode runs. Attend to your immediate impression and keep fixating the dot. Watch for as long as you can maintain your first steady interpretation, and press Space (or the button) as soon as it switches to a second one. Pressing ends the display at once; otherwise it ends after 10.7 s.

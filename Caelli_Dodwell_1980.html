Methods note

Everything below is from Caelli & Dodwell (1980). Amber = not given in the paper (assumption made here). The page shows the display and the response task only.

Scale on this page: 40 canvas px = 1° (assumed; the paper gives no viewing distance, screen size or pixel scale). Display: bright disks/lines on a dark ground (the paper used a Hewlett-Packard P15 phosphor scope, disk luminance 3.14 foot-Lamberts, p. 30; a browser screen cannot reproduce that luminance or the scope's phosphor decay). Durations are counted in display frames (1 frame = 1/60 s = 16.7 ms), so they are rounded to whole frames: 40 ms = 2 frames (33.3 ms); 20 ms = 1 frame (16.7 ms); 50 ms = 3 frames (50 ms); 150 ms = 9 frames (150 ms); 200 ms = 12 frames (200 ms); 300 ms = 18 frames (300 ms); SOA 40 to 400 ms in 40 ms steps = 2 to 24 frames (33.3 to 400 ms, nominal 40 ms = 2 frames). The realised values assume a 60 Hz display.

Methods note

Everything below is from Caelli & Dodwell (1980). Amber = not given in the paper (assumption made here). The page shows the display and the response task only; no paths, predictions or data are drawn.

Scale on this page: 40 canvas px = 1° (assumed; the paper gives no viewing distance, screen size or pixel scale). Display: bright disks/lines on a dark ground (the paper used a Hewlett-Packard P15 phosphor scope, disk luminance 3.14 foot-Lamberts, p. 30; the screen in a browser cannot reproduce that luminance or the scope's phosphor decay). Timings are limited by the browser's display refresh.

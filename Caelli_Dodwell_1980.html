Methods note

Everything below is from Caelli & Dodwell (1980). Amber = not given in the paper (assumption made here). The page shows the display and the response task only.

Scale: screen px/cm and viewing distance come from "Display setup" (default 37.8 px/cm; 57 cm assumed, the paper gives no viewing distance, screen size or pixel scale); every stimulus is drawn in degrees converted at that scale and the canvas is not rescaled by CSS. Display: bright disks/lines on a dark ground (the paper used a Hewlett-Packard P15 phosphor scope, disk luminance 3.14 foot-Lamberts, p. 30; a browser screen cannot reproduce that luminance or the scope's phosphor decay). The display refresh interval is measured at load (median of about 60 requestAnimationFrame intervals); every duration is converted to a whole number of frames and scheduled by counting frames. The line above the canvas lists the realised durations at the measured rate and warns where a paper duration is not a whole number of frames.

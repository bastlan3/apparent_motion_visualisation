Faithful reconstruction of the stimulus/apparatus in McBeath & Shepard (1989), Perception & Psychophysics, 46(4), 333–337. Viewing distance ≈ 80 cm; each shape subtends ≈ 1.5° (rendered here at 32 px/°). Shapes are flashed near the bottom of the field to prime the upward-curved interpretation. Narrow/medium shapes are oriented so their axis of elongation passes through the kinematic centre of rotation; the Pac-Man is kept asymmetric about the vertical. The curved path itself is the observer's percept and is deliberately not drawn — only the real two-flash stimulus and the adjustable window are shown.

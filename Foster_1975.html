Foster (1975) — probe matching of two apparent-motion arrays

Two stacked arrays on a bright 11° field. Lower: A and σ(A). Upper: A, probe B and σ(A). Frame-counted timing at the measured refresh rate; drawn in degrees from the Display setup below.
Look (monocularly) at the centre of the top array (the midpoint between its A and σ(A) bars). Adjust the probe's vertical position and orientation until the motion of the top array is visually indistinguishable, as a set of points (ignoring timing), from the motion of the bottom array. No time limit.

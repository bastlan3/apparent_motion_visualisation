Foster (1975) — probe matching of two apparent-motion arrays

Two stacked arrays on a bright 11° field. Lower: A and σ(A). Upper: A, probe B and σ(A). Display runs at frame-counted timing (60 Hz assumed).
Look (monocularly) at the centre of the top array. Adjust the probe's vertical position and orientation until the motion of the top array is visually indistinguishable, as a set of points (ignoring timing), from the motion of the bottom array. No time limit.

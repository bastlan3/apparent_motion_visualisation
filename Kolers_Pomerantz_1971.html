Kolers & Pomerantz (1971) — Figural change in apparent motion

Two shapes are flashed in sequence on a lit field while the observer fixates a point; the observer responds with button presses during the intercycle interval. Only the physical flashes are shown.

Methods (Kolers & Pomerantz, 1971, J. Exp. Psychol. 87, 99–108). Values from the paper; amber = not stated / my assumption.
Apparatus (p.99)Multichannel electronic tachistoscope; two shapes in sequence with two lighted pauses between. Target area 12.5 × 10.5 cm = 7° × 6° (drawn here as exactly 7° × 6°) at 1 m viewing distance. Lamp rise 1.5 ms, 90% decay 3.5 ms. Field brightness ≈ 4 ft-L. Timing error < 1%.
Stimuli (p.100)Circle, square, triangle, hollow arrow drawn in black ink on white board, contour 1 mm wide (≈ 3.4′), area equated at 1.56π cm². A circle "subtended 43′" (this equals the circle's radius for 1.56π cm², so the page draws it at that area, diameter ≈ 86′). Centres of gravity coincide with Line A or B of Fig. 1. Shapes are outline only. Triangle (isosceles) and hollow-arrow (block arrow with rectangular shaft) proportions are not given in the paper: assumed.
Layout (Fig. 1)Fixation point FP = small neon point source. Fig. 1 dimension labels 3.7°, 1.7°, 0.5° run B→A, A→FP, FP→right edge. Used here: B 1.1° from the left edge, A = B + 3.7° = 4.8°, FP = A + 1.7° = 6.5° (0.5° from the right edge); A and B at 44% of field height, FP at 48% (read from the printed figure). FP size (small dot with glow) and colour (orange, as for a neon lamp) are assumed. All sizes are drawn in degrees (shape sizes: paper cm at 1 m × 0.5729°/cm; field and positions directly in degrees) and converted to pixels from the Display setup.
Timing (Fig. 1, p.100)Shape 1 (25–400 ms; 80 ms in Exp I) → ISI 10, 25, 50, 71, 100, 125, 165, 210, 260, 390 ms (field lit) → shape 2 (same duration) → ICI 3200 ms during which the observer responds → next cycle. Here the refresh rate is measured at load (median of ~60 rAF intervals); every period is converted to a whole number of frames at that rate (nearest, minimum 1) and scheduled by counting frames. The table below is generated by the same function as the trials. Not tachistoscope-accurate (paper: lamp rise 1.5 ms, timing error under 1%).
ViewingLight-adapted right eye only, fixation on FP. 3 min dark adaptation then 1 min light adaptation to a blank field before testing (not reproduced). Extensive practice before each session.
Exp I (pp.100–102)4 shapes → 16 pairs (4 same, 12 disparate); shapes 80 ms each; 10 ISIs; shapes centred on Lines A and B (assumed: first shape at A, second at B). Response: one button if smooth continuous motion was seen, another if anything else. Later part ("complement"): arrow→square, arrow→circle, arrow→triangle.
Exp II (p.104)Right-angled trapezoid (truncated isosceles right triangle), area equal to Exp I shapes, paired with its mirror reflection (M), inversion (I) or 180° planar rotation (R); both members centred on Line A. 5 durations (25, 50, 100, 200, 400 ms; the printed Fig. 4 legend) × 10 ISIs. Response during the ICI: button 1 smooth rotation of a rigid shape, button 2 smooth movement of a plastic shape (the paper gives no further explanation of "plastic"), button 3 neither. Observers were first never told what to see, then given these instructions. Exact proportions and which orientation is shown first are not given. Used here: truncated isosceles right triangle with parallel sides 1 and 0.45 and base 0.55 (45° slanted side), narrow end pointing left first. Default duration 100 ms is arbitrary.
Exp III (p.105)Five trapezoid pairs, each shape 100 ms: large left-pointing with its mirror image; small left-pointing with its mirror image; large left vs small left; large right vs small right; large left vs small right. Large = Exp II area, small = half that area. Superposed (both on Line A) and then separated (one on A, one on B). Response: one button for smooth continuous change of any kind, another if the transition was not smooth and continuous.
Trial structurePaper: ISI and pair sequences counterbalanced within sessions of 600 observations, practice before each session (Exp I: 4 sessions; Exp II: 6 sessions, 24 observations per point; Exp III: 3 counterbalanced sequences, 4 sessions, 24 observations per point). Here one block = every ISI (× every pair, and in Exp II every duration, in Exp III both placements) once in random order, one trial per 2 flashes + ISI + 3200 ms ICI. The paper's actual sequences, the 6 haphazardly chosen disparate pairs of Exp I, and the practice block are not specified; all 12 disparate pairs are offered. Exp III "left-pointing" is assumed to mean the narrow end of the trapezoid points left.
Not specified / not reproducedExp III shape-to-A/B mapping in the separated case is assumed (first shape at A, second at B). Surround and luminance: the paper gives 4 ft-L (about 14 cd/m²) field brightness and 3 min dark + 1 min light adaptation; the monitor here is uncalibrated (use a dim room and low monitor brightness) and adaptation is not enforced. Monocular viewing is not enforced (use the right eye only). Exp II was first run uninstructed; only the instructed version is shown. Responses are recorded in memory and not displayed or scored.

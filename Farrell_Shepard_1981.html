Methods (Farrell & Shepard, 1981, JEP:HPP 7(2), 477–486)
Observers: 8; 3 sessions × 60 recorded trials = 180 recorded trials each, after practice trials and a few warm-up trials per session (p. 479–480).
Stimulus: one polygon at a time, centred. Each of 3 random polygons (A, B, C) has one vertex on each of 18 equally spaced radii (20° apart); two vertices 8 units from the origin in opposite directions define the vertical axis; the other 16 radii are drawn from 1–17 excluding 8, without replacement; adjacent vertices joined by straight lines (p. 479). Symmetric versions: cut along the vertical axis and join the half to a copy rotated 180° (left-side / right-side versions). Intermediate polygons interpolate along each radius ¼, ½, ¾ of the way from asymmetric to symmetric: 0, 25, 50, 75, 100% (27 distinct polygons; each observer saw 15, with left- or right-side versions counterbalanced). Size: ≈ 4.8° visual angle at a “standard viewing distance” (p. 480).
Sequence: alternation between the “upright” orientation (defined by the vertical axis) and an orientation displaced by 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 or 180° clockwise or counterclockwise (fixed within a trial); each view immediately replaces the other, no ISI, so SOA = duration of each view. SOA starts at 1 s on every trial (p. 480).
Response: left/right keys of a response panel change each field's duration by 10% (speed up / slow down; assignment counterbalanced), centre key records SOA₁ then SOA₂, which ends the trial. If rigid rotation could not be seen, observers noted this on a comment sheet. Design: random order, 1 trial per polygon (15) × 12 cw/ccw disparities (180° counted once) = 180 (p. 480).
Page timing: frame-counted at an assumed 60 Hz (16.67 ms/frame). Each view lasts round(SOA/16.67) frames; 1 s = 60 frames; after each 10% step the SOA is re-quantised to whole frames. Realised durations are shown above under “Display”.
Assumptions (not specified in paper): exact radii of A, B, C are shown only in the paper's Fig. 1, so polygons here are seeded random draws following the recipe (placeholders). Speed-up multiplies SOA by 0.9; slow-down by 1.1 (paper says “the same 10% steps”). Minimum SOA 1 frame; no upper cap. Rendered as a green (P31 phosphor) outline, 2 px line, black background, no fill, no fixation point; luminance, line width, viewing distance, refresh rate unspecified. 4.8° taken as the diameter of the circle containing radius 17, at an assumed 40 px/degree (≈192 px). Which view is shown first is unspecified (upright first here). Apparatus: Tektronix 604 CRT, P31 phosphor, dimly lit room.

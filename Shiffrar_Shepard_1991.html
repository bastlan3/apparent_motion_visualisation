Shiffrar & Shepard (1991) — Comparing two successive cube rotations (same / different)

A green wire-frame cube rotates 315° (1,680 ms nominal), a 2 s blank follows, a second cube rotation appears, and the observer presses same or different during that second rotation.
Press next to start a trial. Watch the first rotation and form a mental image of it; during the second rotation press same or different as quickly and accurately as possible. Half of the trials are identical (same rotation twice); on the other half the two rotations differ in some way. Reversals of apparent depth/direction (Necker) do not count as a difference.
Press next to begin.

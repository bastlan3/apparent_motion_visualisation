Khuu, Kidd & Badcock (2011) — Element orientation and visual saltation

Three briefly flashed oriented grating patches (two at one location, one at another) are viewed in the periphery (10° left of fixation); the observer then places an oriented probe where an element was seen (Exp. 1–2), or adjusts a reference motion-path shape against a test display on a moving-dot background (Exp. 3). No percept, path or result is drawn here.

SOA (ms):

Methods (Shiffrar & Freyd, 1993, Psychological Science 4(6), 379-384)

The original stimuli were full-colour photographs of a real model (Figs 1, 3, pp. 380, 382). They are not reproduced here: this page draws schematic placeholder figures (dark clothing, face, textured grey backdrop) whose limb poses only approximate the described pairs. Pose geometry, backdrop luminance and picture size are assumptions.

Shiffrar & Freyd (1993): human-body picture pairs alternated at different SOAs

SOA (ms):

Answer sheet (response interface)

After viewing, the observer indicated the path(s) of the motion, if any, on a paper answer sheet with diagrams, once per SOA level. Here, draw with the mouse on the diagram of the two poses (hand-drawn, nothing is recorded or scored). Several paths may be drawn, or tick "no motion".

Methods (Shiffrar & Freyd, 1993, Psychological Science 4(6), 379-384)

The original stimuli were full-colour photographs of a real model (Figs 1, 3, pp. 380, 382). They are not reproduced here: this page draws schematic placeholder figures whose limb poses only approximate the described pairs.

Chatterjee, Freyd & Shiffrar (1996) — Experiments 1–5: two alternating pictures, "around" or "through"

Schematic stand-ins. The paper used full-colour video photographs of a human model, wooden boards/blocks and a wooden mannequin (Figs 1, 2, 4, 7, 8). The pictures here are flat drawings that reproduce only the layout: the body (or object) stays fixed and one limb (or board) appears at either side of a body part (or block). Pictures, poses and colours are assumptions. Responses are not recorded.

Parameters (paper values)

DisplayMacintosh 21 in. colour display, 1152 × 870 px, 40 × 30 cm screen (p. 918)
Viewing distance80.5 cm (p. 918). Each image 8 cm high × 10 cm wide = 5.68° × 7.08°. Canvas here is 500 × 400 px for that frame (≈70.6 px/deg); physical size depends on your screen.
FramesTwo pictures (A, B) of one pair alternate, each followed by a blank interval (p. 919)
SOA133, 266, 400, 533 ms; stimulus duration = ISI = SOA/2 = 66.5, 133, 200, 266.5 ms (p. 919)
Realised at 60 HzSD = ISI = 4, 8, 12, 16 frames (66.7, 133.3, 200, 266.7 ms); SOA = 8, 16, 24, 32 frames. Frames are counted via requestAnimationFrame, which is assumed to run at 60 Hz.
Presentation lengthOpen-ended: the observer watches as long as needed to determine the path, then clicks a stop button below the pictures (p. 919)
ResponseAfter stop: blank screen with a "Please Respond" button, then a two-alternative choice, "around" or "through" (the inanimate object or the body), then a button to start the next trial (p. 919–920)
Exp 116 observers. Body about body vs body about object; 7 pairs each (limb about foot, ankle, knee, face, waist, seated knee). Every pair at all 4 SOAs; 8 blocks of 7 stimuli (4 per condition) = 28 trials per condition; condition order counterbalanced; pairs and SOAs randomised within condition (p. 918–919).
Exp 216 new observers. Body about body vs object about body (wooden board, limb length, no support); same SOAs and block structure as Exp 1 (p. 921).
Exp 316 new observers. Partial body: only the moving limb and the body part it moves about visible, everything else black; visible region 1.1° × 1.6° (as printed). 2 blocks of 14 trials (7 pairs × 4 SOAs), random order (p. 923).
Exp 416 new observers. Object about object (boards/blocks matching limb and occluder size, location and orientation); 2 blocks of 14 (p. 924).
Exp 516 new observers. Wooden artist's mannequin, enlarged to the same visual angle as the human model; 2 blocks of 14 (p. 925).
Practice4 picture pairs at 133 or 533 ms SOA, different from test items (p. 919)

Not specified in the paper (assumptions here)

Background and blank-interval colour (mid-grey used; Exp 3 surround is black as stated), fixation point (none), refresh rate (60 Hz assumed), which picture comes first (A), number of cycles before stop (unlimited), button labels beyond "Please Respond" and stop, instruction wording, trial order of the practice pairs, stimulus drawings. Practice here uses the first four pairs of the chosen condition as stand-ins. Block mode shows one block at a time: 7 pairs once each at a random SOA (Exp 1–2) or 14 trials, 7 pairs × 4 SOAs (Exp 3–5).

Bundesen, Larsen & Farrell (1983) — apparent transformations of size and orientation

Two views of the same character, differing in size and in rotation in the picture plane, alternate continuously with no blank interval. The observer speeds up the alternation until the impression of one shape-preserving moving object breaks down, then slows it again until it returns.
Free viewing, binocular, no fixation mark. The pair alternates from an initial SOA of 1000 ms. Keys act on single presses (key repeat ignored).
Right key (→): faster (SOA decreased by 1/11), repeat until form-preserving movement disappears (plastic deformation, partial movement, flicker); then press centre key (Space).
Left key (←): slower (SOA increased by 1/10), repeat until form-preserving movement reappears; then press centre key (Space), which ends the trial.

Assumptions / not specified in the paper:
  • Typeface and stroke weight: not given. Characters are drawn here as single-stroke polylines (this page's own simple stroke font for 3, 4, 7, J, P, R) with a constant 2 px line, stretched to the format box; line weight does not change with size or direction.
  • Extent: each character's stroke box (in its own upright frame, height × width) equals the format size: largest 7.9 × 5.3 cm (p. 553), smallest 1 × 0.67 cm (p. 553), middle format 2 × 1.3 cm (assumed from the 1:2:8 ratio, p. 552).
  • Size assignment in unequal-size pairs: the paper (p. 552-553) does not say which view carries the larger size. The page always puts the smaller format at u and the larger at u + v (fmt [0,1] for s = 2, [1,2] for s = 4, [0,2] for s = 8); a per-trial random assignment would be an alternative.
  • Which view comes first: the u view is shown first by default, then the (u + v) view; the paper does not say (a selector allows the other order).
  • Timing: the display refresh period is measured at load (median of about 60 rAF intervals); every duration is a whole number of frames at that rate and alternation is scheduled by counting frames; realised durations are shown in the display line, with a warning when 1000 ms is not a whole number of frames. The SOA is kept as a continuous value (start 1000 ms, × 10/11 per right tap, × 1.1 per left tap) and realised as round(SOA / frame) frames, minimum 1 frame (the paper's hardware had no such minimum). A key tap changes the SOA from the next switch on. Dropped frames are not detected. The 40 Hz raster intensification and P-31 persistence are not simulated.
  • Colour and luminance: green on near-black as a proxy for the P-31 phosphor; not calibrated to 0.6 cd·µs·cm⁻¹ or 0.5 cd/m².
  • Scale: pixels per cm come from the credit-card calibration above (default 37.8 px/cm, assumption) and the viewing distance (default 60 cm, the paper's value); stimuli are drawn in degrees (the paper's cm sizes at 60 cm converted to visual angle). The calibration is stored in this browser under the key am_display.
  • Keys: arrows and Space are this page's choice; the right key in phase 2 and the left key in phase 1 do nothing (the paper does not say). When a trial ends the screen goes blank; in session mode the next pair starts after a blank interval of 1000 ms (assumed; the paper gives no inter-trial interval). Free-pair mode ends on a blank screen.
  • Each observer in the paper saw one pattern only; here character and version are free to change outside a session. Session count/breaks (4–6 sessions) are not reproduced; practice pairs are an assumption.

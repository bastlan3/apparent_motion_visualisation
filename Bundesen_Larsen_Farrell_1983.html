Bundesen, Larsen & Farrell (1983) — apparent transformations of size and orientation

Two views of the same character, differing in size and in rotation in the picture plane, alternate continuously with no blank interval. The observer speeds up the alternation until the impression of one shape-preserving moving object breaks down, then slows it again until it returns.
Free viewing, binocular, no fixation mark. The pair alternates from an initial SOA of 1000 ms. Keys act on single presses (key repeat ignored).
Right key (→): faster (SOA decreased by 1/11), repeat until form-preserving movement disappears (plastic deformation, partial movement, flicker); then press centre key (Space).
Left key (←): slower (SOA increased by 1/10), repeat until form-preserving movement reappears; then press centre key (Space), which ends the trial.
Display calibration: px per cm (canvas is 640 x 480; the largest format, 7.9 cm, must fit: max about 60). Viewing distance in the paper: 60 cm.

Assumptions / not specified in the paper:
  • Typeface and stroke weight: the paper used a point/vector character drawn on a CRT; here the glyph outline of a sans-serif font is drawn as a thin line (about 2 px).
  • Ink extent: each glyph is stretched non-uniformly so its ink bounding box (in its own upright frame, height × width) equals the format size: largest 7.9 × 5.3 cm (p. 553), smallest 1 × 0.67 cm (p. 553), middle format 2 × 1.3 cm (assumed from the 1:2:8 ratio, p. 552).
  • Which view comes first: the first-orientation (u) view is shown first, then the (u + v) view; the paper does not say.
  • Timing: alternation is counted in display frames and assumes a 60 Hz display (16.7 ms/frame). The SOA is kept as a continuous value (start 1000 ms, × 10/11 per right tap, × 1.1 per left tap) and realised as round(SOA / 16.7 ms) frames, minimum 1 frame (the paper's hardware had no such minimum). The realised duration is shown in the experimenter readout. Dropped frames are not detected. The 40 Hz raster intensification and P-31 persistence are not simulated.
  • Colour and luminance: green on near-black as a proxy for the P-31 phosphor; not calibrated to 0.6 cd·µs·cm⁻¹ or 0.5 cd/m².
  • Scale: 1 cm = the px/cm setting at the canvas's nominal size (no CSS shrinking); visual angle is correct only if the screen is calibrated and viewed from 60 cm.
  • Keys: arrows and Space are this page's choice; the right key in phase 2 and the left key in phase 1 do nothing (the paper does not say). When the trial ends the screen goes blank; what happened after termination is not specified.
  • Each observer in the paper saw one pattern only; here character and version are free to change.

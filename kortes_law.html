Methods and status

The Korte (1915) paper was not available, so no value below is taken from it. The only element treated as established (from secondary sources, e.g. Attneave & Block 1973 citing Korte) is that two stimuli at different positions are shown in succession and that their spatial separation and temporal separation are varied. Every other value is an assumption of this page. No response interface, trial structure, observer instruction or condition levels from the paper are reproduced, because they could not be verified.

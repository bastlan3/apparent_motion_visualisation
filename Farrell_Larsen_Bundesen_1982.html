Farrell, Larsen & Bundesen (1982) — apparent rotational movement

Two views of one pattern alternate; the observer adjusts the stimulus-onset asynchrony with three keys. Methods reconstruction: Perception 11, 541–546.
Keys: → right key shorter SOA (×10/11)  ·  ← left key longer SOA (×11/10)  ·  Space / ↓ centre key register a limit.
Press the right key repeatedly until the impression of rigid movement disappears, then register (centre key). Then press the left key repeatedly until the impression of rigid movement reappears, then register again (centre key) — this ends the trial.

Assumed / not specified in the paper
The second curved element named in the Fig. 1 caption is not visible in the printed figure and is not drawn here.
Orientation 0 = upright; positive rotation = clockwise; first member of each pair = orientation u (paper gives neither).
On-time of each member = SOA − 50 ms (implied by constant 50 ms ISI), rounded to whole display frames; ISI = 50 ms in whole frames. The alternation runs until the trial is ended.
Control condition: the first member of each pair is the left pattern, and the control patterns are upright (orientation 0) with the curved element to the left (paper gives neither).
Trial start: the first member appears immediately when the participant starts the trial (no blank or countdown). Between trials the screen is blank (main) or shows only the fixation dot (control). In the control condition the fixation dot (0.1 deg radius here) is drawn from the moment the control list is selected; its size is not given.
Timing: new SOA values take effect at the start of the next on-period. The registered SOA is the nominal value (continuous, multiplied by 10/11 or 11/10 per press); the CSV also logs the realised SOA in frames and ms (computed as whole frames of on-time plus ISI for the nominal SOA at the moment of the key press, i.e. the frame count the next on-period would use, not the on-period already under way) and the measured refresh rate.
Scale: stimuli are drawn so that a 10 mm pattern (size 1) subtends the paper's angle at the paper's distance (1 m main, 0.75 m control; 0.57/1.15/1.72/2.29 deg for sizes 1-4 at 1 m); control displacements are 2, 3, 4 deg and the dot is 1 deg below. Viewing distance defaults to the paper's value for the condition (stored calibration contributes only px/cm). Preview offers sizes 1 and 4 only for the control. Do not zoom or shrink the browser window; the canvas is sized from the calibrated px/cm so large patterns are not clipped, but is not otherwise rescaled.
No limits on SOA are imposed except that on-time is at least 1 frame. Stroke colour (green, P31-like), line width (2 px) and luminance are uncalibrated; 40 Hz intensification is not modelled. Default screen scale 37.8 px/cm until calibrated.
Exact instruction wording, practice trials, the blank between trials (here: next trial starts when the participant presses Start/Enter) and the rest-break format are not given; the instruction text above is a paraphrase. The SOA value is not shown to the participant.
Viewing distance cannot be enforced; angular sizes hold only if you view from the entered distance with the calibrated px/cm.

Farrell (1983) — alternating line figure, SOA staircase

One line figure alternates between two views that differ in location and orientation. The observer shortens the SOA until form-preserving movement is replaced by partial movement and/or flicker, then lengthens it until form-preserving movement returns.
Right key / → shorten  ·  Left key / ← lengthen  ·  Centre key / Space record.
Phase 1: tap shorten until form-preserving movement is replaced by partial movement and/or flicker, then press centre (SOA1). Phase 2: tap lengthen until form-preserving movement returns, then press centre (SOA2), which ends the trial.
Reconstructed task description (wording not reported; based on the p. 86 procedure text): judge whether you see a single object moving back and forth along a path within the picture plane while preserving its shape; press when this gives way to partial movement and/or flicker, and again when it returns.
Press Space to start trial 1.

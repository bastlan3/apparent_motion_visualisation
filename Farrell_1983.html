Faithful reconstruction of Farrell, J. E. (1983), Perception & Psychophysics 33(1), 85–92. A rigid line-figure (a straight line plus curved sector elements) is shown alternately in two positions differing in location and orientation; viewing 1 m, ISI fixed 50 ms, SOA varied via stimulus duration (SOA = duration + ISI). The observer runs a two-sided staircase — lower the SOA until form-preserving movement breaks into partial movement/flicker (SOA₁), then raise it until rigid movement returns (SOA₂); minimum SOA = their mean. Exp 1: rigid rotation about the screen centre at radius R (D = 2R·sin(V/2)). Exp 2: radial object of size L, near end at M = 6−L, far tip at R = 6 cm (D_min = 2M·sin(V/2), D_max = R·V). Exp 3: location and orientation varied orthogonally (horizontal separation D, independent V). The smooth interpolated path is the observer's percept and is deliberately not drawn — only the two real flashes appear. The predicted-SOA readout uses the paper's fitted a, b, c per experiment (Exp 1: 86, 1.59, 32.41; Exp 2: 129, 2.18, 19.52; Exp 3: 75, 0.8, 28.76).

Ramachandran & Anstis (1983) — Extrapolation of motion path

Four dots alternate as two diagonal pairs, shown alone or embedded in two rows of dots flashed one after another (or in two static rows). Fixate the central dot and do not track the motion with your eyes.
Fixate the central dot. Report whether the four central dots appear as streaming (two parallel straight paths) or bouncing (two U-shaped paths). Keys: S = streaming, B = bouncing.
Press Start when ready.

Display setup

Resize the bar to match a credit card (8.56 cm) held against the screen, then set your eye-to-screen distance (default = paper's 100 cm).
